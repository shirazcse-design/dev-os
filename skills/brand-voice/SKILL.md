## Purpose

Help maintain a consistent, clear, and on-brand writing style across product surfaces by reviewing drafts and producing polished copy that aligns with the project's voice and content standards.

## Inputs

- A draft or existing piece of product copy, UI text, page content, or documentation
- Reference files such as docs/design.md and docs/ContractIQ_PRD.md
- Optional context about the intended audience, desired tone, or specific terminology to preserve

## Instructions

1. Read the reference files first so you understand the product's voice, positioning, and terminology.
2. Review the submitted content for clarity, tone, consistency, and alignment with the documented brand direction.
3. Preserve the core meaning while tightening phrasing, removing ambiguity, and making the language more concise and precise.
4. Maintain a restrained, information-forward tone that fits the product's design system and product positioning.
5. When terminology is inconsistent, suggest a preferred term and use it consistently in the revised copy.
6. If the content is incomplete, flag gaps briefly and provide a best-effort revision rather than leaving placeholders unresolved.

## Output

A revised version of the supplied content with:

- clearer and more concise wording
- improved tone and voice consistency
- aligned terminology and phrasing
- brief notes summarizing the main changes made
